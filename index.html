PMT Score Check 2026 — Apna Ghar Apni Chhat: What Happens After You Apply

Applying for Apna Ghar Apni Chhat is only the first step. Once your application is in, your PMT Score Check result is used to verify whether your household genuinely qualifies, and that verification decides whether your name moves forward to the allotment stage. Many applicants hear nothing for weeks and assume the worst, but silence usually means the file is still moving through the queue. Before you read on, make sure you understand the check itself with the PMT Score Check guide on pmtscorecheck.com, so you know what your score means and how to confirm it through 8171.

How verification works after you apply

After you submit the application, the office compares the details you gave with the household record behind your PMT score — your CNIC, family size, income and housing situation. If the two match, the file moves ahead; if something does not match, such as an old address or a family member who has since moved out, the file can be held for correction. This is why the score matters even after you have applied: it is the record the verification is checked against, not just a number you needed at the start.

What the allotment list means

Once verification is complete, eligible households are placed on a list in order of need, and allotment follows that order. Being on the list does not mean a house is ready the next day — allotment depends on how many units are available in your district and how many households are ahead of you. Check the official announcements for your district rather than believing anyone who claims to know the list in advance. If your name is not on a published list, it usually means verification is still under way or your file needs a correction, not that you have been rejected.

If your application is rejected

A rejection normally comes with a reason: the household record does not match the application, the score is above the cut-off for the scheme, or a document is missing. The first thing to do is find out the exact reason from the office, because each one has a different fix. A wrong record can be corrected through a re-survey at the BISP office; a missing document can simply be submitted. What cannot be fixed is paying someone to change the result — no agent can alter your score or your place on the list. The full step-by-step explanation of the verification itself is in the Apna Ghar Apni Chhat PMT Score Verification guide, which covers the check, the documents and the common mistakes to avoid.

A note on staying safe

After you apply, you may get calls or messages from people claiming they can move your name up the list or guarantee allotment for a fee. These are scams. Allotment follows the official verification and the official list, and no one outside the scheme can change either. Never pay anyone who promises a house, never share your CNIC with strangers, and report such offers through the official helpline on 0800-26477.
